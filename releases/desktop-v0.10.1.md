# LiveCam 0.10.1

Released 2026-09-27.

A fix for automatic pitch, and all messages in one place.

### Fixed

- The microphone check could misread your voice's pitch, sometimes by an
  octave, and automatic pitch then made your voice in calls noticeably
  higher or lower than intended. It now measures correctly. Please run the
  microphone check once after updating: until you do, a call reminds you
  that automatic pitch is not set.

### Changed

- All messages now appear in one place, at the top center of the window,
  including the confirmations of your own actions. Confirmations are short,
  clear sentences, for example "La voix a été renommée."
